## Unresolved

1. **Exact encoding floor of the spectra.** ECMWF says values below "approximately 10⁻⁴" are not encoded, but the reference file contains values down to 1.1×10⁻⁷, so the floor is not 10⁻⁴ for this file. Missing bins are set to 0; the energy they omit is not quantified.
2. **Altimeter assimilation.** The ECMWF wave document lists gridded altimeter wave-height fields (140246–140248) but this check did not find an explicit statement that ERA5 assimilates altimeter data. The UI says it is unverified. Read the ERA5 documentation before claiming it.
3. **Delivered wave grid for single levels.** The spectra file is on 0.5°, and ECMWF states that the wave model uses a reduced lat/lon grid of about 0.36° that CDS interpolates to a regular grid. That the single-levels wave variables are also on 0.5° was not checked against a real download.
4. **Hourly availability of the 2D spectra.** The time step is configurable (1, 3, 6, 12, 24 h) but hourly spectra were not requested. 6-hourly is the tested default.
5. **IEC TS 62600-101 naming.** The spectral width `ε0 = √(m0·m-2/m-1² − 1)` and the flux directionality ratio `|J_vec|/J` are labelled by their formulas, not by the standard's names, because the standard was not read.
6. **The 19% group-velocity figure** (10 s waves at intermediate depth) from the brief was not reproduced. The finite-depth code is tested only against its deep and shallow limits and the dispersion relation.
7. **Real CDS downloads.** Option A (single levels) has completed real downloads through this app: two 2025 jobs of 12 monthly requests each. Option B (MARS spectra) has no completed job: five were refused by CDS as too large, one was cancelled, and one 6-hourly July 2026 job holds 16 chunk files covering days 1-31 but was marked failed when an app restart interrupted it; those files have not been opened or analysed. The MARS requests copy the keys of the request that produced `output.nc`. Untested: queue time on long requests, the `oper` stream and the `expver` cut-off of about 100 days.
8. **Option A vs Option B on real data** has not been compared: only a synthetic Option A built from the spectra was used to test the cross-check panel, so its agreement there is by construction.
9. **Hourly vs 6-hourly sampling check** is implemented but has only run on synthetic hourly data.
10. **Dateline.** The CDS form allows longitudes from −360 to 360, so a true wrap-around request may work. The app clips the box at ±180° instead and has not tried a wrapped request.
11. **What CDS counts as cost for spectra.** Probed on 2026-10-01 with `--probe` for -8.885, 114.895 in July 2026: one day at 6-hourly was accepted with `expver` 5 and with `expver` 1, and also for 2020-04-01 with `expver` 1; one day at hourly was refused as "cost limits exceeded". So the time list matters on its own (a 6-hourly month of 86,400 fields works; an hourly day of 17,280 does not), which a pure field-count rule does not explain. The largest number of time steps known to be accepted is 4; intermediate counts (for example 8 or 12) were not tested. The public costing endpoint returns HTTP 500 and the library retries a 500 for up to 500 attempts of 120 s, so the estimate is run only with `--estimate`, never during a download; no estimate was obtained. The fetcher splits on the time axis down to 4 and remembers what CDS accepts.
12. **Variability indices.** MVI and SVI are implemented as Kamranzad, Etemad-Shahidi and Chegini describe them ("Sustainability of wave energy resources in southern Caspian Sea", Energy, 2016): the difference between the maximum and minimum monthly (seasonal) mean wave power, divided by the annual mean wave power. That paper cites Cornett (2008, ISOPE) and Zheng et al. (2013, Ocean Eng.) for the indices and Sanil Kumar and Anoop (2015) for the formula; none of those three was read here. Differences: the annual mean in this tool is the mean of the 12 monthly means, and seasons are calendar seasons. The COV definition (population std / mean of per-record flux) was not found in that paper and was not checked against a source.
13. **Extreme-value results on real data.** The peaks-over-threshold code recovers the generating parameters and the analytic return levels of a synthetic process with a known GPD tail (30 years, three bootstrap seeds). On a synthetic record whose tail is not GPD the fit is unstable and the threshold-sensitivity table shows it; no real long ERA5 record has been analysed. Threshold choice is the user's: the app reports sensitivity but does not select it.
14. **Device assessment against a real device.** Verified with hand-computed matrices and a synthetic example only. The "characteristic width" for capture-width ratios is the user's definition, and the period axis of a real power matrix must be confirmed from its source (the Te/Tp ambiguity is reported, not resolved).
15. **Screening on large downloads.** The effect of stride sampling was measured on a 3-year 6-hourly synthetic spectra record (4,384 steps, 40 nodes). This data had a smooth seasonal cycle, which is the best case for stride sampling. No real multi-year spectra record has been measured. On the synthetic data, differences from the exact computation were: mean Hm0 0.0005 m, mean Te <0.0001 s, mean flux 0.01 kW/m, P95 flux 0.0, and season ratio 0.0014. The current budget computes exactly up to 500,000 node-records (nodes × records) and dynamically falls back to a stride above it.
16. **Browser behaviour of the tool tabs** was exercised in the Claude preview pane only (Chromium-based); other browsers were not tried.
17. **Progress, ETA, resume and probe against real CDS.** These were tested with a fake CDS client that refuses oversized requests; none has been run against the real service. The ETA is the median of the requests downloaded so far, and real queue times varied from about 3 to 8 minutes per request, so it is a rough guide. A resumed job replays the same refusals to reach the same plan, which depends on CDS applying the same cost rule on both runs. If CDS accepted a different shape the second time, the files would not match by name and the month could be downloaded again into overlapping files; the analysis does not check for overlapping records between files.
18. **Real Option B job left as failed.** Job `68158d8157b1` holds 16 chunk files but is marked failed after an app restart. **Resume download** should complete it (it also fetches the model depth), but this was not tried.
